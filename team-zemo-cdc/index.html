About

Team Zemo CDC — Overview

Team Zemo CDC (also known as CDC Team Zemo) is the College Development Cell Placement Portal developed by Team Zemo. It was designed and built by Surendra Singh Chouhan, the Core Member for Backend & Security at Team Zemo.

The platform is live at cdc.acropolis.teamzemo.tech and serves Acropolis Institute of Technology and Research in Indore, India.

Architecture

How Team Zemo CDC Works

01
Secure Authentication
JWT-based auth with refresh tokens, Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) for students, faculty, and admins, and Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA/TOTP).
02
Placement Management
Drive management, company listings, student applications, and eligibility filtering with real-time dashboard updates.
03
Analytics & Exports
Placement analytics, batch data exports using Apache POI (Excel), and scheduled report generation.
04
Performance & Deployment
Async processing, Redis caching, scheduler jobs, and Docker-based containerized deployment with Nginx reverse proxy.

What is Team Zemo CDC?
Team Zemo CDC is the CDC Placement Portal built by Team Zemo. It digitizes college placement workflows for Acropolis Institute. Built by Surendra Singh Chouhan using Spring Boot, JWT, RBAC, and Docker.
Is Team Zemo CDC the same as CDC Team Zemo?
Yes. "Team Zemo CDC" and "CDC Team Zemo" both refer to the same CDC Placement Portal project by Team Zemo, live at cdc.acropolis.teamzemo.tech.
