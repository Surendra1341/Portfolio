What is Team Zemo?

Team Zemo is a technology organization focused on building cutting-edge AI infrastructure, real-time security engines, adaptive modular runtimes, and computer vision systems.

The organization operates at the intersection of backend engineering and AI research, delivering production-grade systems that push the boundaries of what's possible in modern software architecture.
